Applications of ICT in academic environment.
Learning.
1.Online lectures and tutorials.
Advantage: Enables revision at any time.
Disadvantage: Prolonged exposure causes digital fatigue.

2.E-books and research via search engines.
Advantage: Instant access to updated knowledge.
Disadvantage: Promotes plagiarism.

Communication.
1.Class whatsapp group.
Advantage: Real time dessemination to accademic notices.
Disadvantages: Information overload due to irrelevant discussions.

2.E-mail for assignment submission.
Advantage: Paperless and time efficient.
Disadvantage: Dependent on internet connection.

Manangement.
1.Computerized attendance system.
Advantage: Accurate and secure record keeping.
Disadvantage: Vulnerable to system failure.

2.Online result managemeent.
Advantage: Transparent and immediate result publication.
Disadvantage: Requires data security measures.

Entertainment and engagement.
1.Gamified learning plateforms.
Advantage: Increases student engagement.
Disavantage: May divert attention from education.

2.Educational documentaries via projectors.
Advantage: Simplifies complex tasks through visualization.
Disadvantage: Risk of misuse for non-educational purposes.


Task 4: Keyboard-Only Operation.

Procedure:
1.Window + D, to access desktop.
2.Ctrl+Shift+N ,To create folder shortcut.
3.Enter to open directory and Ctrl+Shift+N, to create copyfolder.
4.Application key, to create a new text document.
5.F2 to rename as myname.txt, Enter to open.
6.Enter personal identification and Ctrl+S ,to save.
7.Alt+F4, to exit, Ctrl+C to copy.
8.Arrow keys to copyfolder, Ctrl+V to paste.
9.F2 to rename paste file to backup.txt.
10. Return to dictionary and delete original file.

Result: Operation completed. The original file removed and only backup.txt remains in copyfolder.
